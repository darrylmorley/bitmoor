Before we talk

A few useful details.

Is this a finished enterprise platform?

The consumer apps are available today. Additional workflows, integrations and partnership work are discussed individually. We will confirm what already exists and what would need development.

Which computers and devices are supported?

Bitmoor’s current apps run on macOS. Available readings depend on the app, the Mac and the connected hardware. We will establish whether your intended setup is supported before agreeing work. See the individual apps.

Can WhatCable certify a cable or prove it is safe?

No. WhatCable reads the information the hardware and macOS expose. It can help investigate a setup, but it does not replace electrical testing, physical inspection or an independent certification process.

Will a partnership influence the diagnostic findings?

Findings follow the available evidence. Commercial relationships do not change a diagnostic verdict. Any joint promotion should be clearly identified.
